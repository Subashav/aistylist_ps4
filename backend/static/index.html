The Methodology

HOW AI STYLIST REIMAGINES YOUR LOOK.

A synthesis of Google Gemini computer vision and local diffusion neural virtual try-on calibrated specifically to your physique.

Step 01 / Perception

Undertone & Facial Geometry

Gemini Vision scans your portrait to detect warm/cool skin undertones, face shape, and natural contrast levels to determine ideal complementary shades.

Step 02 / Curation

Fabric & Chromatic Calibration

OpenCV reads precise garment RGB/Hex hues while our recommendation engine curates 4 distinct pairings balancing trousers, footwear, outerwear, and textile drape.

Step 03 / Realization

Photorealistic Virtual Try-On

Local FASHN VTON v1.5 warps and realistically drapes the garment directly onto your photo, rendering natural folds, shadows, and collar structure with zero cloud latency.
